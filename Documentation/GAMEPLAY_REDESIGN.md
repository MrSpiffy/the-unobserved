## Art composition

There are 24 decorative slots, three per wing. The third slot in each wing swaps a compact two-object arrangement; the other slots preserve the previous single-object logic. These are peripheral, non-colliding visual compositions, with proportional fitting and a shared safe envelope. Existing fixed furniture remains the majority of dressing. The layout does not lengthen corridors or change movement speed.

## Remaining playtest questions

The archive and gallery deliberately share the clear-aperture lesson; assess whether the later application still feels repetitive. Observe whether players infer high shielding versus low clearance from the beams, and whether the workshop low approach is readable without coaching. Test island jumps and diagonal approaches manually. Automated circuit solving and targeted movement do not establish human comprehension, every possible shortcut, or absence of all softlocks.
